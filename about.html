01 / ABOUT US

Knowledge begins
with curiosity.

NovaFacts is an educational website focused on science, space, physics, artificial intelligence, technology and fascinating facts about our world.

We created this platform to make interesting information easier to discover and understand. From the smallest details of science to the enormous scale of the universe, there is always something new to explore.

Our aim is to provide readers with an accessible place to learn, think and discover new ideas.
