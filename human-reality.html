The Reality of Being Human

Where did we come from? Who are we beneath our names, fears, dreams and social identities?

Explore the Reality ↓ Questions to Think About

Before we judge the world, let's understand ourselves.

Every human being has a beginning, a body, a mind, relationships and a limited amount of time.

01. The Journey of Being Human

1

We Begin Before We Can Remember

Human development usually begins when a sperm cell fertilizes an egg, forming a single cell called a zygote.

You did not choose your first breath or the place where your life began.

2

Our Bodies Carry a Long Story

Our bodies develop through genes, cellular processes and interactions with the environment.

3

We Learn Who We Are

We learn language, habits, beliefs and social rules through experience.

4

The Mind Is More Complex Than It Looks

Emotions are real experiences, but they do not always tell the whole story.

You entered the world without a finished story. Every day, your actions help write the next part.
A reflection—not a scientific law.

02. The Reality We Often Overlook

The Mask We Show

A confident appearance does not reveal everything someone is experiencing.

The Pressure to Compare

Status can affect opportunities, but it does not measure the full worth of a person.

The Stories We Believe

Good evidence can give us a reason to rethink an old belief.

Our Time Is Limited

We do not need every answer about existence to make meaningful choices today.

03. What Is the Meaning of Life?

Science can investigate how life develops. Meaning also involves philosophy, culture, relationships and personal values.

Meaning can look different for different people.

04. Questions Worth Asking

Am I living by my values or only by others' expectations?

Notice which goals genuinely matter to you.

What evidence would change my mind?

Look for reliable sources and consider alternative explanations.

How do I treat people when I gain nothing from them?

Curiosity and kindness are often wiser than quick judgment.
