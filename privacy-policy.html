Privacy Policy.

Transparency, trust and respect for your privacy. Learn how NovaFacts handles information when you explore our website.

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 10, 2026
Welcome to NovaFacts. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how it may be used, and the choices available to you. By using NovaFacts, you acknowledge this policy.

01. Information We Collect

NovaFacts may collect information that you voluntarily provide, such as when you contact us by email or submit information through a website feature.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, including browser type, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, pages visited and general usage data.

02. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the NovaFacts website.
  • Improve website content, features and user experience.
  • Respond to questions and messages.
  • Understand general website traffic and performance.
  • Protect the website against spam, abuse and security issues.

03. Cookies and Similar Technologies

NovaFacts or services used on the website may use cookies and similar technologies. These technologies can help remember preferences, understand website usage and support advertising.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work as expected if cookies are disabled.

04. Third-Party Advertising

NovaFacts may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including services such as Adsterra or HilltopAds where enabled.

These providers may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to deliver advertisements, measure performance and provide relevant advertising according to their own privacy policies.

NovaFacts does not control the data practices of independent advertising providers. Please review the privacy policies of the relevant providers for more information.

05. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or use third-party services. These websites and services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any third-party website they visit.

06. Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07. Children's Privacy

NovaFacts is an educational website intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that violates applicable privacy laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

08. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings and manage advertising preferences through available provider controls. Contact us if you have a privacy-related request.

09. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or applicable requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

Questions About Privacy?

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the NovaFacts team.
