Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Welcome to NovaFacts. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website, read our articles, or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

NovaFacts does not require visitors to create an account to read its public articles. If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

Our website hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and browser requests, as part of delivering the website and maintaining security.

2. How We Use Information

Information sent to us may be used to respond to questions, address feedback, improve website content, and protect the website. We do not ask visitors to send passwords or other sensitive personal information by email.

3. Cookies and Advertising

NovaFacts may use advertising services in the future. If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is enabled, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, or personalize advertisements, subject to its own privacy policies and applicable settings.

Visitors can review Google's information about advertising and privacy at Google Ads and Privacy .

4. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages and may link to external websites or services. When you visit those services, their own privacy policies and practices apply. NovaFacts does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

5. Children's Privacy

NovaFacts is an informational website. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can review the request.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to handle information responsibly. However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions, please visit our Contact Us page and use the contact details published there.
